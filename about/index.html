About

From the frontline to the system.

I started on a headset in 2011. Today I build AI into customer support at scale. This is how I got here, and what I learned on the way.

The short version

Support is where a company keeps its promises.

I am a fifteen-year customer experience and operations leader. I rose from MTN call-centre representative to Head of Customer Operations at Flutterwave, and I now build AI into support at scale. I say the story plainly and prove it with numbers.

I believe in warm, direct, analytical work: short sentences, honest data, and a lesson in every story.

Connect on LinkedIn →
01The frontline · 2011

I learned customers at MTN.

In 2011 I answered calls as a Customer Care Representative at MTN Nigeria. No dashboard teaches what a frustrated caller teaches you in ninety seconds: people want to be heard, and then they want it fixed.

02The craft · 2015–2022

I learned quality, data and discipline.

At First City Monument Bank and then Tek Experts I moved from service officer to QA supervisor, Lead QA and CX Quality Manager, leading a team of nine quality analysts. Quality, analytics and Lean Six Sigma became how I think.

03The system · 2022–now

I learned to build systems that learn.

At Flutterwave I built the Contact Centre Quality Framework, scaled a multilingual team three times, lifted chat CSAT from 65% to 85%, deployed Intercom Fin AI and now lead Customer Operations. The aim stays the same: systems that think, learn and scale.
