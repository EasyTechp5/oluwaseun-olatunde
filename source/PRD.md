# PRD: Oluwaseun Olatunde Personal Website

Nov 2, 2026 · @Israel

## 1. Overview, vision and objectives

This PRD defines a personal website for Oluwaseun Olatunde, Head of Customer Operations at Flutterwave, that turns a scattered LinkedIn presence into one owned, premium home for his CX, AI and operations leadership.

**Problem.** His proof of impact (a chat CSAT lift from 65% to 85%, a 39% cut in annual operating costs, Intercom Fin AI rollout, a 3x team scale-up) sits across LinkedIn experience entries, reposts and webinars. A visitor cannot see it in 30 seconds, and LinkedIn does not rank for his name or niche on Google.

**Vision.** A calm, confident, editorial-quality site that makes a CEO, a conference organiser or a recruiter think: this is the person to run, fix or scale a customer operation with AI.

**Objectives**

1. Establish Oluwaseun as the go-to African voice on AI-enabled customer operations ("AI is an amplifier, not a foundation").
2. Convert visitors into speaking invitations, advisory and consulting enquiries, board and executive opportunities, and mentorship requests.
3. Rank first on Google for "Oluwaseun Olatunde" and in the top results for "CX leader Nigeria" and "AI customer support operations Africa".
4. Give him a content engine he can run alone in under 30 minutes per week.
5. Look and feel better than any CX leader's personal site in the region.

**Out of scope for v1:** e-commerce, member login, a full course platform, a multi-author blog.

## 6. Design direction, visual identity and motion

**Design concept: "Intelligent warmth."** A premium editorial look that signals analytical rigour (clean grids, big numbers, precise type) while staying human (warm colour, real photography, generous space). It should feel closer to a well-designed annual report than a template portfolio.

**Colour system**

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | #0E1B2C (deep navy) | Text, dark sections, footer |
| Paper | #FAF7F2 (warm off-white) | Page background |
| Signal | #F4A300 (amber) | Primary buttons, highlights, key numbers |
| Teal | #0F7C7A | Links, charts, secondary accents |
| Mist | #E7EDF3 | Cards, dividers |
| Coral | #E4572E | Small alerts and hover accents, used sparingly |

Amber and navy are chosen to echo warmth and trust, and to sit clearly apart from Flutterwave's own brand palette so the site reads as personal, not corporate. Dark mode inverts Paper and Ink and keeps Signal. Contrast of body text against background must be at least 7:1.

**Typography**

- Display: a high-contrast serif such as *Fraunces* or *Playfair Display* for headlines and big numbers.
- Body and UI: *Inter* or *Manrope*, 18px body on desktop, 1.65 line height.
- Numbers: tabular figures in the results strip.

**Layout.** 12-column grid, 1200px max content width, 8px spacing scale, large section padding (96px desktop, 56px mobile), generous white space, asymmetric hero with portrait, occasional full-bleed navy sections for rhythm.

**Imagery.** One professional studio-quality portrait set (formal, relaxed, speaking on stage); real event photos from his webinars and panels; no stock handshake photos. Subtle abstract "network of nodes" line art as a brand motif to hint at systems and AI.

**Motion** (tasteful, never gimmicky)

- Hero text reveal and portrait fade-up on load.
- Counters count up when the results strip scrolls into view.
- Cards lift 4px with a soft shadow on hover; buttons have a gentle arrow slide.
- Timeline line draws as the user scrolls.
- Smooth page transitions; all motion respects reduced-motion settings.

**Component list.** Sticky header with scroll shrink, hero, metric counter, pillar card, case-study card, quote band, timeline, article card, talk card, logo row, testimonial slider (from his seven received recommendations, with permission), form, toast, footer.

**Responsive.** Mobile-first. Breakpoints at 480, 768, 1024 and 1440px. Tap targets at least 44px. The mobile hero stacks portrait above the headline.

**Quality bar.** Lighthouse scores of 95 or higher for Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO on mobile for Home, About and an article page.

**Non-functional requirements**

- **Performance:** Largest Contentful Paint under 2.0 seconds on 4G in Lagos; total page weight under 1.5MB on Home; images in WebP or AVIF with lazy loading; CDN delivery.
- **Reliability:** 99.9% uptime; automated daily backups; free SSL.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA; keyboard navigation, visible focus, alt text, form labels, reduced-motion support.
- **Privacy:** Cookie consent that declines non-essential tracking by default; privacy policy; compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act and GDPR for international visitors.
- **Security:** HTTPS only, security headers, rate-limited forms, CAPTCHA or honeypot, admin protected by two-factor login, dependencies updated monthly.
- **Low-bandwidth friendliness:** Works acceptably on slow mobile networks, since many Nigerian visitors browse on mobile data.
- **Maintainability:** Content editable by the owner; design tokens and components documented so future pages stay consistent.

## 8. Tech stack, SEO, analytics, accessibility, security

**Recommended stack** (chosen for speed, editing ease and a premium result)

| Layer | Recommendation | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Framework | Next.js (React) with Tailwind CSS | Fast, SEO-friendly, strong animation options |
| Animation | Framer Motion | Smooth, accessible motion with reduced-motion support |
| Content | Sanity or Payload CMS (or MDX files in Git for a simpler build) | He edits articles and case studies without code |
| Database and forms | Supabase | Stores enquiries and subscribers; row-level security |
| Email | Resend for transactional mail; Beehiiv or ConvertKit for newsletter | Reliable delivery, double opt-in |
| Scheduling | Cal.com or Calendly embed | No custom build |
| Hosting | Vercel with a custom domain and CDN | Global edge, free SSL, preview deployments |
| Analytics | Plausible or PostHog, plus Google Search Console | Privacy-friendly, goal tracking |
| Monitoring | Vercel analytics and uptime monitor | Early warning |

Alternative for a faster, lower-code launch: build the design in Lovable or Framer and connect a headless CMS later.

**Domain.** Recommended: oluwaseunolatunde.com (confirm availability), with .ng as a defensive redirect.

**SEO plan**

- Target keywords: "Oluwaseun Olatunde", "CX leader Nigeria", "AI customer support operations Africa", "customer experience quality assurance framework", "contact centre AI Nigeria".
- Technical: semantic HTML, schema.org Person, Article and Event markup, clean URLs, canonical tags, image alt text, internal linking between case studies and articles, XML sitemap submitted to Search Console.
- Content: publish two long-form articles per month for the first six months, each built from a LinkedIn post expanded into 800 to 1,200 words.
- Off-page: link the site from LinkedIn (featured section, website field and About), speaker pages and partner sites.

**Analytics and goals.** Track enquiries, call bookings, media-kit downloads, CV downloads, newsletter signups, scroll depth on case studies, and traffic source (LinkedIn versus search versus direct). A monthly one-page report goes to Oluwaseun.

**Security and compliance checklist**

- HTTPS and HSTS enforced; Content Security Policy; secrets stored in environment variables.
- Form protection through rate limiting and a honeypot or Turnstile challenge.
- Admin accounts on two-factor authentication; least-privilege roles.
- Consent banner, privacy policy and a data-deletion contact.
- Permission obtained before publishing any employer metrics, logos or colleague names; a quick review against Flutterwave's confidentiality and social-media policy before launch.
