Deploying Intercom Fin AI

Automation placed on top of a fixed support system, not a broken one. Workforce optimised by 10% with service levels held.

Context

Support volumes grow faster than any hiring plan. Intercom's Fin AI agent and chatbots offered a way to absorb that growth, but only if the knowledge and workflows behind them were sound.

Challenge

AI is an amplifier, not a foundation. Put it on a messy knowledge base and it scales confusion at machine speed. The real work was readying the operation, then proving the automation did not cost customers anything.

What I did

  1. Audited support content and common contact reasons so the AI answered from accurate, current material.
  2. Deployed Intercom Fin AI and chatbots, with clear hand-off rules so customers could always reach a person.
  3. Used agent and customer feedback as a training loop: support trains the AI, not the other way around.
  4. Held response and resolution rates as hard guardrails while optimising workforce by 10%.

Results

Workforce optimisation held through 2024 and 2025: 10%.

Lessons

  • Fix support first, then automate. AI amplifies what is already there.
  • Guardrail metrics are what let a leadership team trust automation.
  • The best training data for AI is your best agents' work.
