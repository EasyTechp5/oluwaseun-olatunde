Revamping a multilingual IVR

Voice support rebuilt in four African languages, lifting voice engagement by 15%.

4
African languages supported
15%
Improvement in voice engagement
ContextChallengeWhat I didResultsLessonsTools

Context

Phone remains a primary support channel for many customers across Africa. The IVR is the front door, and for many callers it was the hardest part.

Challenge

Menus assumed English first, and callers in other languages dropped out or pressed zero. The first minute of a call decided how the rest would go.

What I did

  1. Reviewed the call flow and menu structure to remove steps that confused or lost callers.
  2. Rebuilt the IVR in four African languages so customers could be served in the one they think in.
  3. Rewrote prompts in plain, short sentences and tested them with real callers.
  4. Tracked voice engagement before and after to confirm the redesign worked.

Results

Improvement in voice engagement: 15%.

Lessons

  • Language is access. A customer who cannot navigate cannot be helped.
  • Short, plain prompts beat clever ones.
  • Test the front door with the people who use it.

Tools

  • IVR platform
  • Call-flow design
  • Voice prompts
  • Engagement tracking
