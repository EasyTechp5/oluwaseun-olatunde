Cutting operating cost by 39%

A thoughtful office relocation and transport restructure reduced annual operating cost by 39% in H2 2024.

39%
Reduction in annual operating cost
H2 2024
Delivered in the second half of the year
ContextChallengeWhat I didResultsLessonsTools

Context

Customer operations is a people business, which makes it a large cost line. In 2024 there was a clear case to reduce cost without touching service quality.

Challenge

Cutting cost in support usually means cutting people, and that shows up in customer experience. We needed savings that customers would never feel.

What I did

  1. Reviewed the full operating cost base to separate costs that serve customers from those that do not.
  2. Relocated the office to a more cost-effective setup.
  3. Restructured staff transport arrangements to remove avoidable spend.
  4. Reported savings against service metrics every month to prove quality held.

Results

Annual operating cost reduction, H2 2024: 39%.

Lessons

  • The biggest savings often sit around the work, not in it.
  • Always pair a cost target with a service guardrail.
  • Bring people with you; they find savings you cannot see.

Tools

  • Cost analysis
  • Vendor and facilities review
  • Monthly reporting
