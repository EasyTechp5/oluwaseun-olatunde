Chat CSAT: from 65% to 85%

Customers were waiting hours for a reply. We rebuilt how chat support runs and brought satisfaction up twenty points.

65→85%
Chat customer satisfaction (CSAT)
3 hrs→15 min
First response time
2 days→12 hrs
Time to resolution
ContextChallengeWhat I didResultsLessonsTools

Context

Chat is where Flutterwave customers expect an answer in minutes, not days. When I took ownership of the channel, satisfaction sat at 65% and customers were waiting hours for a first reply.

Challenge

Slow first response and slow resolution fed each other. Customers chased agents, agents re-read long threads, and quality varied from shift to shift. Adding people alone would not have fixed a broken flow.

What I did

  1. Mapped the chat journey end to end and found where time was actually lost between first touch and resolution.
  2. Reworked routing, queues and ownership so every conversation had a clear next step and a clear owner.
  3. Tightened quality coaching around the behaviours that moved CSAT, using conversation reviews rather than random audits.
  4. Tracked response, resolution and satisfaction together every week so one metric could not be improved at another's expense.

Results

Chat CSAT: 65 to 85%.

Lessons

  • Fix the system before you add headcount or automation.
  • Speed and satisfaction are one problem. Customers forgive a hard answer, not a silent wait.
  • A metric only moves when someone owns it every single week.

Tools

  • Intercom
  • CSAT surveys
  • Quality scorecards
  • Weekly operations review
