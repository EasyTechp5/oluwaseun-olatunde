Quality and continuous improvement

The Contact Centre Quality Framework

Quality as an operating system, not a monthly audit. A framework plus an AppSheet app that made evaluations 30% faster.

30%
Gain in evaluation efficiency
15%
Higher customer satisfaction
ContextChallengeWhat I didResultsLessonsTools

Context

Quality assurance at many contact centres is a monthly audit that nobody trusts and agents dread. I wanted a framework that ran every day and helped agents get better.

Challenge

Evaluations were slow, inconsistent and disconnected from coaching. Evaluators spent more time filling forms than listening to customers.

What I did

  1. Designed the Flutterwave Contact Centre Quality Framework: clear standards, a shared scorecard and a coaching cadence.
  2. Built an AppSheet app so evaluators could score, comment and track in one place, from anywhere.
  3. Connected scores to coaching conversations so every evaluation led to a specific next step.
  4. Calibrated evaluators regularly so a score meant the same thing across the team.

Results

Faster quality evaluations: 30%.

Lessons

  • Quality is an operating system, not a monthly audit.
  • Low-code tools can remove friction without waiting for an engineering sprint.
  • A scorecard earns trust when agents can see how to improve it.

Tools

  • AppSheet
  • Quality scorecards
  • Calibration
  • Coaching cadence
