Scaling a multilingual CX team 3x

Tripled the team in a single quarter pair, kept response above 90% and resolution above 80%.

Context

In 2023 Flutterwave needed customer support in more languages and at far greater volume. That meant tripling a multilingual team in months, while customers kept expecting the same standard.

Challenge

Rapid hiring usually dilutes quality: new agents learn slowly, coaching thins out, metrics slip. The brief was to grow fast and keep the numbers where they were.

What I did

  1. Built a repeatable onboarding and training path so every new agent reached the standard on a predictable timeline.
  2. Used quality assurance as the growth engine: calibrations, scorecards and fast feedback from day one.
  3. Staffed by language and demand pattern rather than flat headcount.
  4. Watched response and resolution rates weekly and slowed intake if either wobbled.

Results

Team scale-up, Q2 to Q3 2023: 3×.

Lessons

  • You can only scale what you can measure and coach.
  • Training capacity, not hiring capacity, is the real speed limit.
  • Grow fast without breaking metrics by deciding the metrics first.

Tools

  • Training curriculum
  • QA scorecards
  • Calibration sessions
  • Workforce planning
